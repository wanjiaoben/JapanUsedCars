Privacy and accuracy basis

Case details stay generic until Wan supplies confirmed, publishable facts. We do not publish customer names, private contact details, exact addresses, or payment information.

Do these export cases include customer personal information?

No. The cases are templates that show vehicle type, destination port, process stage, and timeline placeholders without customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or private buyer details.

Are the numbers final?

No. The case numbers are marked as TODO placeholders until Wan supplies confirmed model, port, and timeline details.
