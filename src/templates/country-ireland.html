Ireland import guide

Import Japanese used cars to Ireland

The total cost for importing a used car from Japan to Ireland includes Customs Duty where applicable, 23% import VAT, VRT, the NOx levy, port charges, NCT or registration steps, and inland transport. JUC handles Japan-side sourcing, vehicle information, export certificate preparation and shipping coordination; Ireland clearance, taxes, VRT, NCTS, insurance and local compliance are handled by the buyer or an Ireland agent unless a quote explicitly includes them.

What should you confirm before bidding?

Confirm the exact vehicle, auction sheet, odometer record, export eligibility, destination port, customs agent, Irish tax assumptions and insurance path. Do this before bidding because Irish tax and registration outcomes depend on vehicle-specific documents and Revenue assessment.

What is the Ireland cost stack?

  • Japan-side vehicle, auction or service, export handling and shipping costs.
  • Customs Duty where applicable; it is not fixed at 0% or 10% for every vehicle.
  • Import VAT, currently stated by Revenue as 23%.
  • VRT, NOx levy, port charges, registration steps, insurance and inland transport.

How does VRT work?

Revenue assesses VRT using the vehicle details and the applicable rules. Revenue guidance explains that the VRT amount can include a CO2 component and a separately calculated NOx levy. The payable amount is not a fixed JUC website price.

What documents from Japan are usually checked?

Revenue guidance refers to documents such as the Japanese Export Certificate, CO2 and NOx confirmations from Japanese MLIT where required, customs declaration or MRN records, certified translations and other vehicle-specific evidence. Confirm the document set with your Irish clearing agent before purchase commitment.

What are the timing rules?

Revenue guidance says the NCTS appointment should be made within 7 days of the vehicle entering the State and the vehicle should be registered within 30 days. Late registration can lead to additional VRT or other consequences under current rules.

What about insurance and immobiliser checks?

Irish insurers and local compliance providers may ask about security equipment, immobiliser status, model specification and replacement parts support. Do not assume every Japanese import has the same insurance outcome; check with your insurer before purchase commitment.

How much does it cost to import a used car from Japan to Ireland?

The total depends on the vehicle and Japan-side fees, ocean freight and optional insurance, Customs Duty where applicable, 23% import VAT, VRT, the NOx levy, port charges, agent fees, NCTS or registration costs, insurance and inland transport. Without vehicle-specific inputs and Revenue assessment, JUC cannot guarantee an exact landed total.

Can JUC handle Irish registration or VRT for me?

No. We support Japan-side sourcing, vehicle information disclosure, export certificate preparation and shipping coordination. Irish customs clearance, VRT, NOx levy, NCTS appointment, registration, insurance and local compliance are handled by the buyer or an Ireland agent unless a specific quote explicitly includes them.

Is Irish import VAT currently 23%?

Revenue states that import VAT is charged at the standard rate of 23%. The final tax treatment depends on the import declaration, customs value, vehicle documents and Revenue assessment.

Is Customs Duty always 0% or 10%?

No. Customs Duty depends on classification, origin evidence and the applicable rules at import. EU-Japan Economic Partnership Agreement treatment may require acceptable origin evidence, so duty should be checked with the clearing agent before bidding.

What documents from Japan are usually needed?

Irish registration and tax assessment may rely on the Japanese Export Certificate, CO2 and NOx confirmations from Japanese MLIT where required, customs declaration or MRN information, translations and other vehicle-specific documents requested by Revenue or NCTS.

How is the NOx levy charged?

The NOx levy is a separate component of VRT. The amount depends on the vehicle category, verified NOx emissions at the time of manufacture and the rules in force. For Japanese imports, formal MLIT emissions confirmation may be used. If acceptable NOx data is not available, a flat charge may apply. Revenue or NCTS determines the final amount from the vehicle documents; JUC does not guarantee the tax amount.

How quickly must I start Irish registration steps?

Revenue guidance says the NCTS appointment should be made within 7 days of the vehicle entering the State and registration should be completed within 30 days. Late registration can lead to additional VRT or other consequences under current rules.

Should I check insurance before bidding?

Yes. Cover for Japanese-specification models, immobiliser or security equipment, parts availability and insurance conditions can vary by model and insurer. Buyers should confirm with an Irish insurer before purchase commitment. This does not mean every Japanese import lacks an immobiliser, and JUC does not guarantee that insurance will be available.

Are the example models current inventory?

No. The examples are sourcing examples only. They do not represent current inventory, and actual year, grade, condition, price and export eligibility are confirmed per inquiry before purchase commitment.
