United Kingdom import guide

Import Japanese used cars to the UK

UK imports usually involve an import declaration, VAT and customs duty, NOVA notification, vehicle approval where applicable, DVLA registration, vehicle tax and insurance. JUC handles Japan-side sourcing, auction and condition information, export documents and shipping coordination; UK clearance, modifications, testing, registration and insurance are handled by the buyer or a UK agent unless a quote explicitly includes them.

What should you confirm before bidding?

Confirm the exact model, grade, auction inspection sheet, mileage record, export eligibility, destination port and whether you have a UK customs agent or importer ready. Japanese auction sheet disclosure is useful before commitment, but it does not replace UK import, approval or registration checks.

What is the UK import process?

  • Arrange import declaration and pay any VAT and customs duty that apply.
  • Notify HMRC using NOVA, normally within 14 days of the vehicle arriving in the UK.
  • Complete the applicable vehicle approval route before registration where required.
  • Register and tax the vehicle with DVLA, then arrange insurance before road use.

What changes if the car is under or over 10 years old?

UK vehicle approval depends on the exact vehicle and current rules. GOV.UK states that a vehicle over 10 years old might not need approval, while newer imported cars commonly need an approval route such as IVA before registration. Treat age as a check point, not a blanket exemption.

What preparation points are common for Japan imports?

Common checks include an MPH-readable speedometer, rear fog lamp, headlamp aim, VIN or statutory plate, emissions evidence and other equipment required by the chosen approval or registration path. Requirements are vehicle-specific; meeting a preparation checklist does not guarantee approval.

Can JUC handle UK registration for me?

No. We support Japan-side sourcing, vehicle information disclosure, deregistration, export certificate preparation and shipping coordination. UK customs clearance, taxes, approval, modifications, registration, insurance and local compliance are handled by the buyer or a UK agent unless a specific quote explicitly includes them.

Do Japanese imports to the UK always need the same modifications?

No. Requirements depend on the vehicle, age, equipment and the approval route. Common preparation points include an MPH-readable speedometer, rear fog lamp, headlamp aim, VIN or statutory plate and emissions evidence, but the final decision belongs to the UK authority or test route.

Do vehicles over 10 years old need UK approval?

A vehicle over 10 years old might not need vehicle approval, but this must be checked for the exact vehicle and current UK rules. It still needs import, tax, registration and insurance steps before use on UK roads.

What costs should I check before bidding?

Check the vehicle cost basis, auction or service fee, Japan-side export handling, ocean freight, marine insurance if quoted, UK import declaration, VAT, customs duty, approval or testing, modifications, port and agent charges, DVLA registration, vehicle tax and insurance.
